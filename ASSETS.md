# Jamsil portfolio scene assets

The scene geometry in `public/data/jamsil.json` is a snapshot of streets, pedestrian paths, the Seokchon Lake outline, and building footprints and heights around Jamsil Station, Lotte World Tower, and Lotte World Mall. Source: [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), licensed under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). `scripts/extract-jamsil.py` documents the conversion from OSM XML to local scene coordinates. The scene is an artistic cyberpunk interpretation of these geographic features, not a survey-grade digital twin. OSM credit appears in the scene UI.

The active scene uses selected GLB models in `public/seoul-assets/` from these CC0 packs on 3DAssets.dev: [Bus Station and City Transit](https://3dassets.dev/packs/bus-station-and-city-transit), [Car Park and Road Vehicle Fleet](https://3dassets.dev/packs/car-park-and-road-vehicle-fleet), [Street Food Market and Food Trucks](https://3dassets.dev/packs/street-food-market-and-food-trucks), and [Korean Hanok Village and Street](https://3dassets.dev/packs/korean-hanok-village-and-street). GLB filenames are the source asset IDs; all models are stored locally.

Vehicle IDs were checked against the embedded GLB scene names: `32522` is the slowly moving licensed taxi, `32486` and `32492` are parked cars, and `34225` is a fixed street litter bin.

The road diffuse texture is from [Poly Haven Asphalt 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_01), licensed CC0. `app/coffee-chat/JamsilScene.ts` generates the building facade tiles, Lotte World Tower silhouette, Korean signs, lake surface, and street markings in Three.js.

The upper sidewalk and stair pavers use [Poly Haven Pavement 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/pavement_05); the lower promenade uses [Poly Haven Pavement 06](https://polyhaven.com/a/pavement_06). Both are CC0 2K diffuse and normal maps stored locally in `public/textures/`.

The East Lake promenade, park wayfinding, benches, trees, lighting, and cafe storefronts are original stylized geometry. Their placement follows the OSM lake outline and the description of Seokchon Lake's East Lake, walking trails, rest areas, Lotte World Tower views, and nearby cafe street in the [official Visit Seoul guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions--/Seokchon-Lake-Park/ENP001136). The details are an artistic interpretation rather than exact replicas of park fixtures or individual businesses; the shop names are fictional.

The park's vertical layout is based on Songpa District's description of separate [lake-circling and upper park walking paths](https://council.songpa.go.kr/kr/news/bbsPress.do?flag=subject&key=4f21295da10e6d16e0e1872eda9669ff75e8633e09fe03488380078e1ad69a22&keyword=&pageNum=1388&reform=view), its reference to a [lower walking path with resilient surfacing](https://www.songpa.go.kr/child/detailInfo.do?key=3626&resrceCd=TR0147-1000053), and a [Seoul city photograph of the East Lake north entrance stairs](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1504240). The scene places the upper sidewalk beside Songpa-daero, a stair descent through a retaining wall, a lower lakeside loop, and water below the path. The 2.15-unit drop and exact stair location are inferred for this playable block; they are not surveyed elevations.

`public/textures/seokchon-blue-hour-panorama.png` is the exterior scene's distant backdrop. It is an AI blue-hour adaptation based on Christian Bolz's real [Seokchon Lake in Seoul panorama](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seokchon_Lake_in_Seoul.jpg), with the former generated dusk image used only as a lighting reference. The adaptation adds dusk lighting and changes the photographed skyline and vegetation; it is an artistic interpretation, not an unaltered location photograph. The adapted image is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), the original photograph's license. The scene UI links to the original and identifies the adaptation. The separate 3D far bank now contains a low path and trees instead of the former invented high-rise frontage.

`public/textures/foliage-cluster-v2.png` is a transparent foliage card created with the built-in ImageGen tool in `photorealistic-natural` mode. Final prompt: “One irregular, airy cluster of real deciduous tree leaves attached to thin natural twigs, photographed as a clean isolated cutout for a game foliage card. Numerous small individual leaves with visible gaps and fine twigs, viewed from the side at eye level, in soft overcast blue-hour light. Deep summer green, muted sage, a few desaturated warm leaves, realistic veins and bark. Genuinely transparent background and preserved alpha; no ground, sky, text, frame, painterly or low-poly style.” The card is arranged in rotated 3D clusters around modeled branches.

`app/coffee-chat/TowerLobby.ts` is a stylized Three.js cafe. The sliding entrance doors, fluted coffee counter, warm wall and ceiling lighting, bronze trim, ceiling ribs, espresso machine, pastry case, mugs, and side shelves are authored geometry. `app/coffee-chat/LobbySkyline.ts` replaces the former flat window panorama with layered Three.js buildings, facade window textures, nearby towers, and a gradient sky. The city visible from the cafe is a fictional skyline for depth and atmosphere, not a verified view from Lotte World Tower. The floor uses [Poly Haven Marble 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01). The cafe uses 1K glTF versions of [Modern Arm Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01), [Coffee Table Round 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/coffee_table_round_01), [Potted Plant 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02), [Bar Chair Round 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/bar_chair_round_01), [Croissant](https://polyhaven.com/a/croissant), and [Standing Chalkboard 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/standing_chalkboard_01). These Poly Haven assets are CC0; `scripts/download-lobby-assets.mjs` records the download source and local layout. The scene cuts under a fade from a lake view to the tower vestibule, then opens the sliding doors and moves into the cafe. This is a cinematic portfolio transition, not a verified representation of the tower's real entrance or interior.

`public/lobby-assets/fictional-host-seated.png` is a generated transparent full-body image of a fictional Korean developer host, not a likeness of the portfolio owner or another real person. It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this portfolio and placed as a camera-facing seated character in the 3D cafe. Prompt: “A friendly fictional Korean man in his late 20s or early 30s with a relaxed genuine smile and kind eyes, naturally seated on an invisible low lounge chair, holding a ceramic coffee cup. Soft oatmeal knit cardigan, white T-shirt, dark trousers, clean sneakers. Realistic cinematic game character, believable anatomy and hands, full body including shoes, transparent background, no chair, table, floor, text, or logo.”

Previous scene experiments are excluded from the Git repository; only assets used by the current portfolio are committed.
