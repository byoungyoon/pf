# Seoul atlas geography and credits

The active scene is `app/coffee-chat/SeoulAtlas.ts`, with geographic helpers in `SeoulGeography.ts` and `SeoulTerrain.ts`. `CommuteScene.ts` and `MetroJourney.ts` create the moving subway opening; `CityAssets.ts` instances local free building models. The former walkable Jamsil scene, cafe and their tracked assets have been removed. Ignored local experiments are independent of this scene.

## Geographic data

- `public/data/seoul.json`: Seoul's 25 district outlines from [southkorea/seoul-maps](https://github.com/southkorea/seoul-maps), based on KOSTAT's **2013** municipal boundaries. Copyright Lucy Park; Apache 2.0, with the license in `public/data/seoul-boundaries-LICENSE.txt`. These are historical boundaries, not a claim about current administrative precision. The same file contains 7,565 OpenStreetMap road paths and a legacy river centerline (the centerline is no longer rendered).
- `public/data/seoul-details.json`: 2026-10-08 [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) extracts with 24 water polygons, 967 park/garden/forest/plaza/open-space polygons and 12,979 building/part footprints. Licensed under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The Han River uses complete relations **152336** and **3769500**; relation **18367965** supplies the Saetgang channel. Closed inner rings preserve islands, including Bamseom, and the Seokchon Lake islands.
- `public/data/seoul-terrain.json`: [Mapzen Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), sourced from USGS SRTM/GMTED2010 global elevation data. See the provider's [source and attribution notes](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md). Zoom-11 Terrarium pixels are decoded as `R*256 + G + B/256 - 32768`, bilinearly sampled onto a **431 × 341** grid at approximately 100 m spacing, and rounded to metres. These are modified, resampled elevations; water surfaces are flattened for rendering. Credits appear in the scene footer.
- `public/data/seoul-commute.json`: OSM way **261477607**, the Dangsan Railway Bridge alignment, accessed 2026-10-08; OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The train follows a smoothed version of these nine coordinates. The train interior, eight carriages and bridge detailing are original simplified geometry; deck elevation and animation timing are illustrative, not an engineering model or a service timetable.

The data files are coordinate-converted and filtered extracts, with their source metadata retained. The local map origin is 126.978° E, 37.5665° N. One horizontal scene unit is about 100 m. Both terrain and building heights use metres / 50, giving the **same 2× vertical exaggeration**; this makes ridges legible without separately inflating tower heights.

## Reference and interpretation

[Esri World Imagery](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer) was inspected as a satellite reference for river bends, mountain ridges and urban extent. It is **not redistributed**, rendered as a texture or requested at runtime. The palette and restrained circular composition take inspiration from [Earth 2050](https://2050.earth/); none of its source code or assets are used.

Conversation composition references: [Mindela hero by jishad hussain](https://dribbble.com/shots/26681545-AI-Chat-bot-Website-Hero-Section) and [Arounda's chat assistant landing page](https://dribbble.com/shots/26241203-Landing-Page-for-AI-powered-Chat-Assistants). Their headline spacing, quiet background and distinct input surfaces informed the layout; no reference images or design assets are shipped. Public Pinterest search was also used, but direct boards/pins returned access restrictions. The owner-provided screenshot remains the reference for a centered translucent composer.

Real footprints are used around City Hall/Gyeongbokgung, Namsan, Hongdae/Yeonnam, Yeouido, Gangnam Station, COEX and Jamsil/Seokchon Lake. IFC, Parc One, 63 Building, City Hall and palace structures retain their recorded footprints. Explicit OSM heights are preferred; recorded floor counts are converted at 3.4 m per floor, while missing heights are estimated as low buildings. Building parts retain recorded minimum heights where available. Roofs/facades and bridge elevations remain simplified, and this is not a surveyed city replica.

Lotte World Tower and N Seoul Tower use authored silhouettes at their geographic positions. Heights follow the official descriptions: [Lotte World Tower, 555 m](https://www.lottecon.co.kr/Medias/tower_intro_stability) and [N Seoul Tower, 236.7 m](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Namsan%20Seoul%20Tower%20/ENP000036). Their widths and detailing are simplified for the atlas. N Seoul Tower sits on sampled Namsan terrain rather than an invented cone.

Outside the detailed API extract areas, instanced [Kenney City Kit Commercial 2.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) models suggest the city's density. These are **CC0** assets: 19 detailed buildings/skyscrapers, 14 low-detail buildings and their original palette texture in `public/atlas-assets/kenney/`; the original license is included as `LICENSE-commercial.txt`. Footprints, heights, rotation and tint vary, with procedural boxes retained as a loading/failure fallback. They are excluded from recorded water/open spaces and steep or high terrain. These filler buildings are schematic, not actual buildings at those addresses. Place descriptions are invitations to explore Seoul, not claims that portfolio projects took place at those locations.

## Reproduce

1. Download the KOSTAT GeoJSON from `southkorea/seoul-maps/kostat/2013/json/seoul_municipalities_geo_simple.json`, and an OSM/Overpass `out geom` response for the Seoul road network (`motorway`, `trunk`, `primary`). Run `node scripts/extract-seoul.mjs districts.geojson roads.json`.
2. Fetch OSM API `/api/0.6/map?bbox=west,south,east,north` XML for the eight detailed rectangles listed in `SeoulGeography.ts`, plus `/api/0.6/relation/{id}/full` for the three water relations above. The extracts can include additional small rectangles to complete edge geometries. Run `python3 scripts/convert-seoul-osm.py details-input.json area1.osm area2.osm ...`, then `node scripts/extract-seoul-details.mjs details-input.json`.
3. Run `node scripts/extract-seoul-terrain.mjs /private/tmp/seoul-terrain-tiles` to download/cache the public Terrarium tiles and regenerate the height field. The script uses `sharp`, provided by the installed Next.js dependency tree.
4. Fetch OSM `/api/0.6/way/261477607/full`; retain the way's ordered node longitude/latitude pairs in `seoul-commute.json` with the source and access date. The railway geometry is independent of the building extracts.
5. Download Kenney's Commercial 2.1 ZIP from the linked asset page. Copy `building-a` through `building-n`, `building-skyscraper-a` through `-e`, and `low-detail-building-a` through `-n` from `Models/GLB format/` into `public/atlas-assets/kenney/`. Also copy `Models/GLB format/Textures/colormap.png` into the matching `Textures/` subfolder: these GLBs reference the external texture. Retain the pack's license.

OSM snapshots can change when regenerated. Incomplete relation members are omitted rather than replaced by invented outlines. Commit the resulting data and keep source attribution with any redistributed extract.
